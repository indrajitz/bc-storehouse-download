Changelog

What changed in each build, and why. Written for whoever has to work out whether the version they are running explains what they are seeing — so the entries say what you would notice, not what the diff said.

2.0.0 13 October 2026Latest

The v2 line: the Qt window, the whole implementation roadmap (sends, versions, kits, tagging, previews, tools and courses), archives that unpack on import, and a copy that works when sent outside the studio.

  • Render starts rendering. Pressing Render on an asset (or Render previews in the menu or palette) now renders it straight away on a machine that has Houdini with Redshift — and HDRIs anywhere. Before, it only joined a waiting list and you had to find Render waiting previews here as well. Elsewhere it still waits, and says it is waiting for a machine with Houdini and Redshift.
  • Sending the app to someone now gives them picture tagging. The tag probes and the measured thresholds are built into the app — a copy unzipped elsewhere used to have neither, so picture tagging was off and the safety rule that pictures never set surface tags was missing.
  • The picture model downloads when there is no studio share. On first start the app asks once ("Download the picture model? 373 MB"); the download is checked against the model's exact size and SHA-256 before it is used. On a studio machine it is still copied from the share, silently. Settings › Import & tagging › Get the model now works anywhere too.
  • HDRI viewer: Dome → Cinema 4D / Houdini sit beside Add to kit, so the controls fit a 1440-wide window again.
  • Model details: UVs that run a hair past 0–1 say so, instead of "not a UDIM layout".

1.58.0 13 October 2026

Looking closer, rendering previews, and courses.

1.57.0 12 October 2026

Finding things by how they look, and the work around a project.

New

  • Find similar. Right-click, or Find similar in the panel: what looks like this asset, most alike first — even on a machine without the picture model. Drop a picture on the search field to find what looks like it. Settings › Import & tagging builds the look-alike index (once; then only what changes).
  • Searching for a look ("worn wood") also lists pictures that look like it, after the name matches and marked as picture matches.
  • Look-alikes (Jobs & health): pairs that may be one thing twice — same files, or all but the same picture — to compare, and Keep left / Keep right, which says what it will do first (the other to the trash, kits pointed at the one kept, its tags added).
  • Sends are answered. After Send to Cinema 4D or Houdini, Storehouse says what was built — or what was not and why — and the Send card says which application is open. Install the helpers again (Settings) to get this.
  • Projects (Jobs & health, palette): choose a Cinema 4D or Houdini scene and see every file it uses — which library asset and version, from outside the library, or missing. Package for travel copies the scene and everything it uses into a new folder, relinked, with a manifest, and checks the copy.
  • Take a kit offline to work away from the share; sends use the copies. Back on the share, Offline copies compares them: refresh, add your changes as a new version, or decide where both sides changed.
  • Source & licence in Full details: vendor, source, when and how it was acquired, licence, what it may not be used for, and licence documents kept with the asset. Sends and kit copies mention restrictions.
  • Proposals. Propose tags or report a problem without changing what everyone sees; reviewers accept or decline from the panel, and everything stays on record. Settings can make your tag changes proposals.
  • Most used sort, from what is opened, sent and confirmed built on this machine (kept 180 days; Settings can clear it).

1.56.0 12 October 2026

Finding, comparing and checking things — the everyday part.

New

  • Quick look. Space shows a big picture of the asset; the arrow keys go through the grid, Enter opens it, C or H sends it, Space or Esc closes it.
  • Resolution and "made for". Two new menus: Size (1K, 2K, 4K, 8K and up) and Made for (Cinema 4D, Houdini, 3ds Max, any 3D application...). Picture sizes are read in the background the first time the app opens — about half a minute for the whole library — and then only for what is new.
  • Smart collections. Untagged, and Needs review (picture tags nobody has checked), in the sidebar. They combine with a category, and the panel says why an asset is in the list.
  • When nothing matches, it offers a spelling that would ("Search “concrete”"), the same words across the whole library, or clearing the filters.
  • Compare. Select two to four assets and press Compare, or open an asset's versions and press Compare versions: pictures and details side by side, with what differs marked.
  • It opens where you left off — same view, filters and selection. Settings › General can turn that off.
  • Tiles. A compact setting; Pin, C4D and Houdini buttons on the tile under the cursor; NEW, NEEDS REVIEW and MISSING FILE badges. The list view shows resolution, version, size and file types.
  • The import review says more: names already taken or clashing within the drop, files already in the library under another name (with Skip duplicates), materials missing a map, another renderer's copy folded in, read-only files.
  • The Inbox. An import can wait in the Inbox for someone to look before it shows up in All assets and search; Publish moves it to its category. Or import straight into a kit.
  • Contact sheets of a selection, a kit or the whole view, as a PDF or PNG pages, ready to print or send.
  • Kits say who made them and who changed them last, sit in the sidebar, and Pull a copy shows what it will create — latest versions or all of them — before it copies anything.
  • Jobs & health shows the free space on the share and here and the index backups; the trash shows where each item was and how big it is.
  • Settings › Backups: how many backups to keep and how often, and whether a change made by somebody else is re-read on its own.

Fixed

  • Saved filters forgot their search words (and their sort order); a search could not be saved on its own words at all.
  • "Somebody else changed the library" stopped working after a rebuild from the new window — the rebuild left out what that check compares. Both rebuild buttons now do the same full, backed-up re-read, alone.
  • The selection bar stayed up over an empty grid; the counts on the category pills ignored the other filters.

1.55.0 12 October 2026

The picture tagger, measured honestly — and trusted accordingly.

Changed

  • The picture no longer puts a surface tag on by itself. Tested on vendor packs it had never seen, it named the surface right 65% of the time, not the 92% quoted before (that test had seen near-copies of the same packs). So a surface from the picture is now a suggestion, offered only when it is fairly sure; you pick it or not. Surfaces read from the name, the files or vendor metadata are applied as before, and look, environment and form tags from the picture are unchanged.

Fixed

  • "indoor" on assets that were not. The words of the library's own folders (BC Storehouse, Resources) were being read as evidence about an asset; only folders inside the asset count now.

For whoever maintains the tagger

  • tools/tagger/DECISION.md — which model, how far it is trusted, where it should run, and the measurements behind each.
  • A benchmark of 300 assets for a person to check (benchmark_set.py review), the whole tagging pipeline scored against it (evaluate.py), and processor / graphics card / studio worker compared (compare_devices.py).
  • retrain.py retrains the probes, promotes one only if it is no worse, and can roll back.

1.54.0 12 October 2026

Faster tagging that never holds anything up, undo, and nothing lost while the library or the tagger is away.

New

  • Undo. Ctrl+Z, or the Undo button that appears in the status bar after a change, takes back the last change to tags, a name or a description — from the panel, the edit screen, a review or Tag untagged assets. If somebody else has changed the same thing since, it says who and leaves it. Each asset keeps its last 20 changes in its own asset.json.
  • The picture tagger uses the graphics card — about four times faster on an RTX card, the same answers — except while Cinema 4D or Houdini is open, when it leaves the card to them. It lets go of its memory after five minutes unused.
  • A studio tagger. storehouse worker on one machine serves the picture model to everyone else; set them to "The studio worker" in Settings › Import & tagging. A machine that cannot reach it uses its own.
  • Nothing is looked at twice. What the model worked out for a picture is kept, on each machine and in the library's _cache folder.
  • Work that waits. Files dropped while the library is offline open in Import when it is back. An import while the tagger cannot run goes ahead with the words, and the pictures are looked at later.

Fixed

  • Retry ran without anyone listening. A retried job now reports back to the screen that started it. The Jobs button says how many are running and failed.
  • Sending twice before Cinema 4D picked up the first queued the same material twice. It is sent once.
  • "form: 0% correct" in Settings: that facet was never measured, not wrong. Each facet now says how many held-out assets its figure rests on.

1.53.0 12 October 2026

One tagging answer, wherever you ask, and a record of where every tag came from. Everyone should be on 1.52.0 or later before this goes out: an older copy saving an asset would drop the record this version keeps.

Changed

  • Every screen gets the same tags for the same asset. Import, the asset panel, the edit screen, "Suggest tags…" for a selection and Tag untagged assets all ask one tagger now; they used to have three sets of rules.
  • No more weak guesses. Nothing the picture model is less than 60% sure of is shown anywhere — the "maybe fabric 55%" and "best guess 18%" chips are gone. When it looked and was not sure, it says so in words.
  • The name wins arguments. When the picture disagrees with the name (a ceramic glaze the model calls metal), the disagreement is said in a note under the suggestions, not offered as a tag you could add by accident.

New

  • Where each tag came from. Hover a tag on an asset to see whether it came from the name, the vendor's metadata, the picture (with its score), or a person — and who, and when. Kept in the asset's own asset.json.
  • "Not this". Right-click a suggestion and it is not offered again for that asset — unless the asset is renamed, its picture changes, or a new picture model is sure of it. Taking a tag off an asset counts the same.
  • Review a selection with a preview. "Suggest tags…" says how many tags it will add to how many assets before it does, and can stop suggesting what you untick.
  • Known from the files: formats, maps and resolutions, shown apart from the guessed tags.
  • Not duplicates. In Organise › Near-duplicates, two tags that look alike but mean different things can be marked so, for everyone.

For whoever looks after the tagger

  • tools/tagger/feedback.py adds up what people kept and took off, per source and per picture tag — the tagger's real agreement with the studio. Training (tools/tagger/benchmark.py) no longer learns from picture tags nobody reviewed.

1.52.0 12 October 2026

Groundwork: the roadmap's Phase 0, and one fix that has to reach everyone before anything new is recorded per asset.

Fixed

  • An older copy of Storehouse erased what a newer one recorded. Saving an asset kept only the fields that build knew about, so anyone who had not updated would wipe whatever a newer version had added to that asset's asset.json. Unknown fields are now kept and written back unchanged. Please update: the next version records where each tag came from.
  • Nothing could restore the index from a backup, though one is taken every day and before every rebuild. storehouse backup --restore latest puts the newest back (close the app first); the index it replaces is set aside, not overwritten, and a damaged backup is refused.

New

  • storehouse backup (take one now) and storehouse backup --list.
  • STOREHOUSE_TAGGER_THREADS caps the picture tagger's CPU threads, for a machine that needs its cores for Cinema 4D or Houdini at the same time.
  • A test or measurement with its own app-data folder runs as its own instance instead of waking the window you are working in.

For whoever looks after the app

  • tools/measure_baseline.py measures a machine: start-up, browsing, import, the tagger (also as a 2-core PC), install size. Results for the RTX 5090 workstation are in docs/baseline/; the same command on the RTX 3060 machine gives comparable numbers.
  • tools/smoke_installed.py checks the installed build starts, draws the library, logs no errors and closes cleanly.
  • DESIGN-COVERAGE.md re-checked against all 19 design references.

1.51.0 12 October 2026

The reliability release: the nine problems found in the 11 October audit, and what testing them turned up. Each one has a test that fails on 1.50.1 and passes here.

Fixed — nothing lost

  • A failed export destroyed the archive it was replacing. Export now writes to a temporary file and swaps it in only once it is complete; a failure leaves the previous archive exactly as it was.
  • Sending twice before Cinema 4D or Houdini picked up the first lost the first. Every send is now its own request in a queue, and each is built once, oldest first. Helpers installed before this version still work — they now get every waiting send, not only the last — but install the helper again in Settings so each send is taken in turn; Storehouse says so when you send. A send nobody built within three days is set aside, not built, the next time the application opens.
  • Sending a material that has versions sent the original maps too, so it could be built with the old base colour. Only the latest version's maps go now, plus original files that version does not replace.
  • The viewer opened the wrong file — a larger old version of a model, or an EXR lying beside it. It opens the latest version, of the asset's own kind.
  • A record deleted while you were editing it was quietly recreated from your copy. Saving now refuses, as it already did when somebody else had changed it.
  • Importing into a library that had gone away — the share dropped, or the folder moved — built a new, empty library folder where it had been. It now stops before anything is copied.

Fixed — saying what actually happened

  • Tagging that failed said it had worked. The panel, Suggested tags and Tag from previews now report every asset that could not be written — a read-only asset.json, say — and the panel puts the tag box back to what the asset really has. One locked record no longer stops a batch of hundreds.
  • An empty suggestion list now says why: the picture tagger is not installed, its model is not on this machine yet, pictures are not checked for this category, there is only a data map (normal, height) to look at, the tagger failed (with the error), or it looked and was not sure.
  • A tag typed on the import screen but not yet added was dropped when you pressed Import or clicked another row. It now goes on the row it was typed for. Adding more files to the list also left the tag editor showing a row nobody was editing, so anything typed there went nowhere.
  • A crash dialog when suggestions arrived after their screen had closed — opening another asset while one was still being looked at. The late answer is dropped.
  • An interrupted record write left asset.json.tmp in the asset folder. It is removed now; the record itself was never at risk.
  • 546 "missing file" errors were never missing files (fixed in source as 1.50.2, never released on its own). All were the Synology NAS's own thumbnail files (@eaDir/…@SynoEAStream): older imports copied them in and listed them in 103 assets' records. The files are still on disk — the health check skips @eaDir when it lists an asset's files, then compared the record against that list, so every one looked missing. They are no longer reported, and imports no longer bring NAS files (@eaDir, Thumbs.db) in. A read-only rescan of the library: 0 missing-file errors, down from 546.

1.50.0 11 October 2026

The tagging, redone: one tag box, the same everywhere

The asset panel, the Edit screen, the import screen and Tag selection each had their own way of entering tags — and none let you type a tag that was not already in the list; the Edit screen had no box to type in at all. Now all four use the same tag box:

  • Your tags as chips — click × to take one off.
  • Type a tag and press Enter. It completes from the tag list as you type. Plain words work: wood finds surface:wood, timber finds it too, several at once with commas. A word in two groups (e.g. houdini) asks which.
  • A new word asks once which group it belongs to, with the asset's own group first, and joins the tag list for everyone. A near-miss (rubberr) asks whether you meant the existing rubber first.
  • Suggestions underneath, one click each: from the name, the file names, the vendor's tag file, and — with Look at the picture too — the picture, with its percentage. A tag you take off is not offered straight back.
  • In the asset panel it saves as you go. On the Edit screen it saves on Save (anything still typed included); on the import screen, on Import.
  • Tag selection uses the same box: it shows the tags the selected assets share; what you add goes on all of them, what you take off comes off all of them.

Fixed

  • The asset panel was wider than its column for assets with long file names (since 1.48), cutting off the preview, the Add and Houdini buttons and the Details values. Long names are shortened in the middle, in full on hover.

1.45.0 10 October 2026

Fixed

  • Auto-tagging missed most vendor names. Names written together — WoodFine24, RustPlain004, woodflooring059 — were read as one word and tagged nothing. They are split now. Material words in Italian, German, French and Spanish are recognised too, so ANODIZZATO is metal. Thirteen screens still had the old layout, and Tk was the reason several of them could not be built as designed: no inline video for Setups and Courses, no letter-spacing, no animation, and a grid that had to be hand-drawn on a canvas to scroll at a usable speed. Importing, editing, Send to Cinema 4D and Houdini, jobs, trash and settings are still the old window's. The new one says so plainly rather than offering a button that does nothing. Pinned and Recent are listed but not wired.
